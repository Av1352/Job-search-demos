## Zero-Shot Pipeline

**1. Vision-Language Understanding:**
- CLIP/ViLT models understand images + text together
- No retraining needed for new scenarios
- Describe what to look for in natural language

**2. Action Recognition:**
- Temporal models detect activities from frame sequences
- Works on any activity type
- Confidence scoring per action

**3. Object Tracking:**
- Track people, vehicles, equipment across frames
- Multi-object tracking without class-specific training
- Persistent IDs across occlusions

**4. Insight Generation:**
- Aggregate detections into meaningful metrics
- Identify patterns and anomalies
- Generate natural language summaries
