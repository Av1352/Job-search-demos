## What This Does

AI financial wellness platform with personalized budget recommendations.

**Features:**
- Spending analysis (100 transactions, 8 categories)
- AI budget recommendations (personalized per category)
- Trend visualization (daily spending patterns)
- Savings calculator (15% reduction target)

**Example:** $4K monthly spend → Top category: Food $800 (20%) → AI recommends $600 budget → Save $200/month = $2.4K/year
