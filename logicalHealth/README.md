## What It Does

AI platform that instantly answers insurance coverage questions with 100% accuracy by analyzing policy documents, fee schedules, and benefit structures—eliminating guesswork for patients and providers.

**Example:** Patient needs MRI → Staff asks "Is this covered?" → AI analyzes policy in 2.8 seconds → Returns: "YES - $50 copay, deductible applies, prior auth required" → Complete cost breakdown provided → No more 15-minute hold times
