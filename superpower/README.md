# ⚡ Superpower - Personal Health OS

Production demo of preventive health "super app" with comprehensive biomarker testing and AI-powered personalized insights.

🔗 **[LIVE DEMO](https://vxanju-demos.streamlit.app/superpower)**

## What It Does

Health super app providing biannual testing of 100+ blood biomarkers across 21 categories (hormones, longevity, inflammation, nutrients), centralizing medical records/wearables/genetics, and delivering AI-powered personalized health protocols with 24/7 concierge care.

**Example:** Patient pays $499/year → Biannual lab testing (100+ biomarkers) → AI analyzes results + fitness trackers + medical history → Superpower Score: 87, Biological Age: 38.5 (vs 42 chronological) → Identifies: inflammation elevated, Vitamin D low → Personalized action plan → 24/7 AI concierge available

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Healthcare is reactive (wait until sick), standard physicals miss 90% of biomarkers  
**Solution:** Comprehensive testing + AI insights enable proactive, preventive care

**ROI:** Early disease detection, personalized optimization, $499/year membership
