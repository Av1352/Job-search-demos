## 🎯 What This Does

Automated contract intelligence platform demonstrating:

### 📄 Contract Analysis
- **Risk detection**: Unfavorable terms, missing clauses, compliance issues
- **Key term extraction**: Parties, dates, amounts, payment terms, obligations
- **Risk scoring**: 0-100 scale with severity-weighted assessment
- **Clause coverage**: Which standard clauses are present/missing
- **Issue prioritization**: Critical, High, Medium severity levels

### 💡 Negotiation Support
- **Market standards**: Compare your terms vs industry benchmarks
- **Actionable recommendations**: Specific language to propose
- **Leverage identification**: Where you have strongest negotiation position
- **Playbook generation**: Step-by-step negotiation strategy

### 📊 Contract Intelligence
- **Entity extraction**: Automatically identify key contract elements
- **Clause classification**: Map to standard contract types
- **Compliance checking**: Legal and regulatory requirement verification
- **Comparison tools**: Benchmark against thousands of contracts

## 💼 The Problem: Contract Review is Broken

### Current State (Manual Legal Review)
- ⏰ **3-7 days** for lawyer review
- 💸 **$500-2000/hour** for legal fees
- 🎯 **Hit-or-miss quality**: Depends on lawyer's experience
- 📊 **No benchmarking**: Don't know if terms are market-standard
- 🚫 **Small companies locked out**: Can't afford $5K-20K per contract

### Cost of Bad Contracts
- **Unfavorable terms**: Cost 10-30% more than market standard
- **Missed risks**: Unlimited liability, one-sided indemnification
- **Delayed deals**: Weeks in legal review = lost revenue
- **Litigation**: Bad contracts → lawsuits ($50K-500K+)

### Who This Affects
- **Startups**: Limited legal budget, sign bad deals
- **Scale-ups**: Review 100+ contracts/year, can't afford lawyer for each
- **Enterprises**: Procurement teams need fast turnaround
- **Individuals**: Employment contracts, consulting agreements

## 🔬 Demo Features

### Analyze Contract Tab
**3 pre-loaded example contracts:**

**1. SaaS Vendor Agreement** (High Risk)
- **Issues**: Unlimited client liability, Net 15 payment (too fast), one-sided indemnification
- **Risk Score**: ~40-50 (High Risk)
- **Recommendations**: Cap liability, extend to Net 30, mutual indemnification

**2. Employment Agreement** (Medium Risk)
- **Issues**: 2-year non-compete (too long), broad IP assignment
- **Risk Score**: ~60-70 (Medium Risk)
- **Recommendations**: Reduce non-compete to 6-12 months, limit IP to work product

**3. Consulting Agreement** (High Risk)
- **Issues**: Net 60 payment, no termination rights, unlimited liability, broad IP
- **Risk Score**: ~30-40 (High Risk)
- **Recommendations**: Multiple critical issues to negotiate

**Analysis includes:**
- Risk score (0-100 with color coding)
- Number of issues by severity
- Key financial terms extracted
- Important dates identified
- Detailed issue list with recommendations
- Risk distribution pie chart
- Clause coverage analysis

### Market Standards Tab
**Industry benchmarks for:**
- **Payment Terms**: Net 30-45 days standard
- **Liability Cap**: 1-2x contract value
- **Termination**: 30-90 day mutual notice
- **Confidentiality**: 2-5 years post-termination
- **Indemnification**: Mutual obligations
- **IP Rights**: Clear ownership delineation

**Includes:**
- Negotiation tips for each clause
- Importance ratings (Critical/High/Medium)
- Radar chart comparing your contract to market
- Visual benchmarking
