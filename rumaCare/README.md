## What This Does

AI platform that automates prior authorization submissions, processing requests in 2.1 hours vs 3.4 days manual, achieving 89.9% approval rate and 96.8% automation across 50+ insurance payers.

**Features:**
- Auto-extract clinical data from EHR
- AI-generated medical necessity letters
- Auto-submit to 50+ payer portals
- Real-time status tracking
- Automated appeals for denials
- 96.8% end-to-end automation

**Example:** MRI prior auth request → AI extracts patient data from Epic → Analyzes clinical notes → Generates medical necessity letter → Submits to UnitedHealthcare portal → 2.3 hours total (vs 4 days manual) → 89.5% approval probability
