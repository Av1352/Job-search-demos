## What This Does

AI financial advisor that analyzes portfolios, assesses risk, and generates personalized investment strategies.

**Features:**
- Portfolio analysis with risk scoring
- Current vs target allocation comparison
- Personalized rebalancing recommendations
- 30-year growth projections
- Tax optimization strategies
- Daily rebalancing vs quarterly manual
- Fee comparison (0.25% vs 1% traditional)

**Example:** $500K portfolio (60% stocks, 20% bonds) + moderate risk tolerance → AI recommends rebalancing, projects $1.8M in 20 years at 7.8% return, suggests tax-loss harvesting to save $8.5K/year
