## What This Does

AI platform that automatically generates insurance denial appeals with clinical justification, medical literature, and payer-specific strategies, achieving 73.5% overturn rate and recovering $890K monthly.

**Features:**
- AI appeal letter generation (97.2% automation)
- 73.5% overturn success rate (vs 58.3% manual)
- Clinical NLP for medical necessity
- PubMed literature integration
- Payer-specific strategies
- $890K monthly revenue recovery

**Example:** Denied MRI claim ($8,500) for "not medically necessary" → AI analyzes clinical notes → Searches clinical guidelines → Generates appeal with 3 citations → Predicts 68.5% success → Submits to UnitedHealthcare → Overturned in 14 days → $8,500 recovered
