## What This Does

Population health analytics and clinical decision support for EMR platforms.

**Features:**
- Risk stratification (250 patients: Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- Population health dashboard (avg age, high-risk count, ER visits)
- Individual patient profiles (conditions, medications, utilization)
- Priority alerts (critical patients, high ER use, overdue visits)
- Clinical recommendations (care coordination, medication review)

**Example:** 250 patients → 47 high-risk identified → Recommends care coordination for top 5 → Prevents ER visits

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Healthcare is reactive (wait for ER visits = expensive crisis care)  
**Solution:** Proactive population health (identify risks early, intervene before crisis)

**ROI:** 30-40% reduction in preventable hospitalizations, $500-1000 saved per high-risk patient/year
