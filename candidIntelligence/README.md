## What It Does

Multi-agent AI platform that automates pre-construction workflows for energy infrastructure—reconciling document inconsistencies, chasing missing information, and operating legacy ERP systems to collapse 2-year timelines.

**Example:** Energy project starts → Pre-construction phase normally: 2 years (bidding + engineering + procurement) → AI agents: reconcile inconsistencies across thousands of docs → Auto-chase missing info → Operate legacy software → Coordinate dozens of contractors → Goal: collapse to months

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Energy projects take 4 years total, with 2 years consumed by pre-construction before first construction starts  
**Solution:** Multi-agent AI automates coordination, reconciliation, and legacy system operation

**ROI:** Collapse pre-construction timeline from 2 years to months, billions in cost savings
