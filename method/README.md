## What This Does

AI debt portfolio analysis and payoff optimization.

**Features:**
- Liability tracking (30 debts, 5 categories)
- Risk scoring (multi-factor algorithm)
- Payoff optimization (avalanche method)
- Consolidation analysis (savings calculator)

**Example:** $180K total debt, 15% avg interest → Avalanche payoff: saves $18K vs snowball → Consolidation at 8.5%: saves $12K/year

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Multiple high-interest debts = overpaying thousands in interest  
**Solution:** AI optimizes payoff strategy, identifies consolidation opportunities

**ROI:** $12K+ annual interest savings, 30% faster payoff, better credit score
