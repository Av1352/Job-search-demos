## What This Does

Computer vision platform for home fitness with 3D pose tracking at 60 FPS, real-time form correction, and AI coaching analyzing millions of reps to detect technique mistakes.

**Features:**
- 3D pose estimation (25 keypoints, 60 FPS)
- Real-time form correction (<50ms latency)
- Joint angle analysis (8-12 checks per exercise)
- Auto rep counting
- Voice feedback
- Injury risk detection

**Example:** Squat workout → 3D camera tracks 25 joints → Detects knee cave error → Voice cue: "Knees out!" → Form score 88% → Correction applied → Score improves to 94%
