## What This Does

AI health companion that helps patients manage chronic conditions through daily check-ins, medication reminders, symptom tracking, achieving 94.5% adherence rate and 34.5% improvement, reducing healthcare costs by $2.43M monthly.

**Features:**
- Daily health check-ins with AI
- Smart medication reminders (94.5% adherence)
- Symptom tracking and analysis
- Personalized health insights
- 5 chronic conditions supported
- Wearable device integration

**Example:** Diabetes patient checks in daily → Reports glucose 142 mg/dL, took morning med, missed evening dose → AI analyzes: adherence 87.5%, glucose trending down → Sends reminder for evening med → Suggests reducing lunch carbs → Tracks 30-day improvement: glucose -8 mg/dL, adherence +6.2%
