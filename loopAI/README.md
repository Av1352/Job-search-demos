## What This Does

AI delivery optimization for food delivery platforms.

**Features:**
- Route optimization (multi-stop batching, traffic-aware)
- 24-hour demand forecasting (ML predictions, 94% accurate)
- Restaurant analytics (5 restaurants, demand by cuisine)
- Cost analysis (traditional vs AI-optimized routing)

**Example:** 15 orders in moderate traffic → Traditional: 487min, $120 → AI: 317min, $90 → Saves 170min + $30 (35% faster, 25% cheaper)
