## What This Does

AI health coaching platform creating fully personalized fitness and nutrition plans, achieving 83.4% goal success rate (vs 42.3% generic apps) with 79.3% adherence across 14,563 active users.

**Features:**
- Personalized workout programming
- Custom nutrition plans (macro-based)
- Daily AI coaching and accountability
- 5 goal types (weight loss, muscle gain, sleep, stress, nutrition)
- Wearable integration
- 83.4% goal achievement rate

**Example:** User: 185 lbs, goal 170 lbs in 12 weeks → AI creates: 4 workouts/week (strength + HIIT), 1,850 cal/day (500 deficit), 140g protein → Tracks progress: -4 lbs in 3 weeks, 87.3% adherence → Adjusts plan based on sleep/stress → 78.5% predicted success
