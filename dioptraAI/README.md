## What This Does

AI-powered platform for **contract analysis, risk scoring, and negotiation guidance**.

**Features:**
- Risk detection (unfavorable terms, compliance issues)
- Key term extraction (dates, parties, payment terms)
- Clause coverage & severity scoring (Critical/High/Medium)
- Actionable negotiation recommendations
- Market benchmarking and playbook generation

**Example:**  
Upload a contract → NLP parses clauses → risk scores & issues identified → negotiation support recommendations generated
