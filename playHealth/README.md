## What This Does

AI-powered perimenopause care platform providing personalized treatment plans, symptom tracking, and virtual care, achieving 67.8% symptom improvement and reducing time-to-treatment from 45 days to 3.2 days for 8,240 women.

**Features:**
- AI symptom assessment and tracking
- Personalized treatment plans (HRT, lifestyle, CBT)
- Virtual specialist consultations
- 3.2-day time to treatment (vs 45 days)
- 67.8% symptom improvement
- 92.3% HRT effectiveness

**Example:** Woman (47) reports hot flashes, night sweats, sleep disruption, mood changes → AI assesses severity → Recommends: HRT (estradiol patch + progesterone) + lifestyle (sleep hygiene, exercise) + CBT for mood → Connects with specialist in 3.2 days → 6-month outcome: 67.8% symptom reduction, QoL 3.1 → 4.2/5
