# 🌻 Sunflower - AI-Powered Sobriety Platform

Production demo of addiction recovery platform with AI sponsor "Sam" and integrated tele-therapy clinic - the "Duolingo for addiction."

🔗 **[LIVE DEMO](https://vxanju-demos.streamlit.app/sunflower)**

---

## What It Does

Consumer app + tele-therapy clinic that helps people overcome addiction through AI sponsor support, sobriety tracking, peer community, CBT-based content, and licensed therapist access.

**Example:** User struggling with alcohol → Downloads app → Meets Sam (AI sponsor bee) → Daily check-ins + craving management → Visual sobriety tracking → Joins supportive community → Access to therapy when needed → 87 days sober average, 68% success rate

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** 464M people globally struggle with addiction, but treatment is supply-constrained (not enough therapists)  
**Solution:** AI sponsor provides infinitely scalable 24/7 support + optional human therapy when needed

**ROI:** 68% success rate, $1M ARR in 10 months, 100K MAU in 6 months
