## What It Does

AI platform that transforms employee benefits management through unified data intelligence, automated optimization, and 6 specialized AI agents—cutting costs, reducing admin burden, and improving employee satisfaction.

**Example:** 500-employee company with $1.61M benefits cost → AI analyzes plans, benchmarks against 10K companies → Identifies $254K savings opportunities → Recommends HDHP+HSA switch, vendor consolidation, wellness redesign → Implements in 90 days → 15.8% cost reduction + 420 hrs/month saved
