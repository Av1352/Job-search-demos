## What This Does

Voice AI demo that simulates an enterprise phone agent handling common customer-service calls end to end.  

**Features:**
- Natural, context-aware dialogue for 5 intent types (account, billing, tech support, product, scheduling)  
- Intent classification, real-time STT/LLM/TTS loop, and escalation paths to humans  
- Live sentiment tracking to adapt tone and responses  

**Example Flow:**  
Select a call scenario → run simulated conversation → see transcript, intents, sentiment over time, and resolution outcome.  

---

## Why It Matters

Human call centers are expensive, hard to scale, and suffer from inconsistent quality.  

This demo highlights how AI phone agents can:  
- Cut cost per call while providing 24/7 coverage  
- Reduce handle time and increase first-call resolution  
- Maintain consistent, high-quality customer experiences at scale  

---

## Demo Features

**Call Simulation tab:**
- 5 preconfigured call types with different goals and difficulty  
- Per-call stats: duration, resolution flag, sentiment curve  

**Analytics tab:**
- Aggregated metrics (daily call volume, resolution rate, average handle time, CSAT-style score)  
- Volume and performance by intent category
