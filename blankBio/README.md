## What This Does

AI system that predicts RNA drug properties and clinical trial success before expensive wet lab work.

**Features:**
- RNA sequence analysis (structure, GC content, binding sites)
- Drug candidacy scoring (stability, suitability for therapeutics)
- Toxicity prediction (safety assessment)
- Bioavailability estimation (delivery potential)
- Clinical trial success probability (30%+ vs 10% baseline)
- Phase-by-phase trial predictions

**Example:** Upload mRNA vaccine candidate (90 nucleotides, 52% GC) → AI predicts: High stability, Low toxicity, 82% bioavailability, 42% trial success (3x industry avg) → Recommend fast-track pathway

## Prediction Pipeline

**1. Structure Analysis:**
- Calculate GC content (stability indicator)
- Identify secondary structure motifs (hairpins, loops)
- Predict binding sites for drug targets
- Assess overall stability

**2. Drug Properties:**
- Toxicity risk (low/moderate/high)
- Bioavailability score (delivery potential)
- Stability score (degradation resistance)
- Drug candidacy (0-100%)

**3. Clinical Prediction:**
- Success probability for each trial phase
- Cumulative success rate (preclinical → FDA)
- Development timeline estimate
- Regulatory pathway recommendation

## Why RNA-Based AI Works

RNA is more predictable than small molecules:
- **Sequence determines structure** - AI can learn folding patterns
- **Fewer off-targets** - Specificity via Watson-Crick pairing
- **Faster design cycles** - In silico prediction → synthesis
- **Better for AI** - Clear sequence-to-function relationship

**Result:** ML models achieve 85%+ accuracy on RNA property prediction vs 60% for small molecules.
