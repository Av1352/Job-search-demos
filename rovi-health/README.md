## What This Does

AI-powered healthcare navigation system that helps employees get the right care, at the right place, at the right time.

**Features:**
- Symptom checker with smart triage (ER vs urgent care vs PCP)
- Provider search with real-time availability and in-network filtering
- Benefits explainer with cost transparency
- Company impact dashboard ($580K savings per 1,000 employees)
- 24/7 access, 2-minute average response time

**Example:** Employee has sore throat + fever → Symptom checker recommends urgent care (not ER) → Shows 3 nearby options with wait times + copays → Saves company $220 per visit

## Care Navigation Logic

**Triage Algorithm:**
- Severe symptoms (chest pain, breathing difficulty) → 🚨 Call 911
- High urgency (high fever, severe pain) → ⚡ Urgent Care
- Moderate urgency (persistent symptoms) → 👨‍⚕️ Primary Care
- Low urgency (minor symptoms) → 🏠 Self-care + monitor

**Provider Matching:**
- In-network filtering (auto-applied)
- Distance sorting (closest first)
- Availability check (real-time slots)
- Cost estimation (copay + deductible)
