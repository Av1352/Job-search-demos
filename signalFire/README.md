## What This Does

Investment intelligence workspace that scores startups, surfaces recommendations, and tracks portfolio performance.  

**Features:**
- Multi-factor AI scoring: growth, unit economics, market, team, PMF, capital efficiency  
- Recommendations: Strong Buy / Buy / Hold / Pass with thesis text  
- Portfolio analytics: ARR, growth, sector mix, and IRR-style summary  
- Visuals: score breakdown, growth vs ARR matrix, runway and risk views  

**Example Flow:**  
Pick a sample startup → view AI score and rec → inspect metrics and score breakdown → compare against other portfolio companies.  

---

## Why It Matters

Traditional VC relies on limited deal flow and manual, partner-specific heuristics.  

This demo illustrates how an AI-first approach can:  
- Expand deal universe from hundreds to tens of thousands of companies  
- Standardize evaluation with transparent weighted scoring  
- Give partners synthesized theses instead of raw spreadsheets  

---

## Scoring Framework

**AI score (0–100)** combines 6 pillars:  
- Growth rate (30%)  
- Unit economics (20%)  
- Market size (15%)  
- Team quality (15%)  
- Product-market fit (10%)  
- Capital efficiency (10%)  

Each sample company shows:  
- Stage, ARR, YoY growth, runway, basic multiples  
- Score radar chart and 12‑month revenue projection  
- Short investment thesis (strengths and watchouts)
