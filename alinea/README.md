## What This Does

AI investment platform with portfolio tracking and personalized recommendations.

**Features:**
- Portfolio analysis (10 stocks, 5 sectors)
- AI recommendations (rebalancing, diversification)
- Performance tracking (gains/losses per stock)
- Sector allocation visualization

**Example:** $20K portfolio, 60% Tech → AI flags concentration risk → Recommends reducing Tech to 30%, adding Healthcare/Financial → Better diversification = lower risk

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Young investors don't know how to diversify or when to rebalance  
**Solution:** AI analyzes portfolio, gives actionable recommendations

**ROI:** Better risk-adjusted returns, reduced concentration risk, investment education
