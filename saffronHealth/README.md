## What This Does

Automated referral platform that handles document generation, prior authorization, faxing, and coordination in 8.5 minutes (vs 90 min manual) with 96.8% automation, achieving 92.8% completion rate and saving 420 hours monthly.

**Features:**
- Auto-generate referral letters (98.7%)
- Auto-submit prior auth (94.5%)
- Auto-fax to specialists (99.2%)
- Patient notifications (99.8%)
- Appointment coordination (87.3%)
- 700 specialist network

**Example:** Cardiology referral → AI extracts clinical summary from EHR → Generates referral letter → Submits prior auth to Aetna → Faxes to Dr. Chen → Notifies patient via SMS → Schedules appointment → 8.5 minutes total vs 90 minutes manual
