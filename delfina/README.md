# 🤰 Delfina - AI-Powered Maternal Health

Production demo of pregnancy risk prediction and monitoring platform with AI-powered clinical decision support.

🔗 **[LIVE DEMO](https://vxanju-demos.streamlit.app/delfina)**

---

## What It Does

AI platform that predicts maternal health risks during pregnancy, enabling proactive interventions to prevent complications and improve outcomes.

**Example:** 32yo pregnant patient, week 28 → Monitors vitals, labs, symptoms → AI detects elevated BP trend + glucose risk → Predicts 0.82 preeclampsia risk → Auto-recommends GTT + MFM referral → Intervention prevents complication → 32.5% maternal mortality reduction

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Maternal mortality in US is rising, many complications are preventable with early detection  
**Solution:** AI predicts high-risk conditions before they become emergencies

**ROI:** $5.79M savings per 10K pregnancies, 32.5% maternal mortality reduction, 24.3% preterm birth reduction
