# 🧬 The Generation Lab - SystemAge™ Longevity Diagnostics

Production demo of epigenetic aging test measuring biological age across 19 organ systems using DNA methylation.

🔗 **[LIVE DEMO](https://vxanju-demos.streamlit.app/theGenerationLab)**

---

## What It Does

Longevity diagnostics platform measuring biological age progression across 19 organ systems (cardiovascular, immune, metabolic, brain, reproductive, etc) using 460 biomarkers from blood/cheek swab to detect disease risk before symptoms appear.

**Example:** 45yo patient → Blood draw → AI analyzes 460 biomarkers → Biological age: 41.8 years → Cardiovascular system: 39.8 years (excellent) → Musculoskeletal: 47.3 years (needs attention) → Personalized intervention plan → Track improvement over time

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Traditional healthcare is reactive, disease detected after symptoms appear  
**Solution:** DNA methylation analysis detects "biological noise" predicting disease propensity early

**ROI:** Preventive care enables early intervention, 275+ clinic partners worldwide
