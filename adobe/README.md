## What This Does

Three specialized AI agents collaborate to build complete marketing campaigns.

**Agents:**
- 🔵 **Audience Agent** - Segment analysis, channel recommendations
- 🟣 **Content Agent** - Headlines, email copy, social content
- 🟢 **Optimizer Agent** - A/B tests, metrics, performance predictions
- 🎯 **Orchestrator** - Coordinates agents, synthesizes strategy

**Example:** "AI Fitness App" → Audience finds millennials on Instagram → Content creates workout posts → Optimizer designs CTR tests → Unified campaign strategy
