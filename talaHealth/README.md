# 🏥 Tala Health - AI-Powered Virtual Care Platform

Production demo of 24/7 AI healthcare platform combining multi-agent systems with licensed clinicians for complete patient journey management.

🔗 **[LIVE DEMO](https://vxanju-demos.streamlit.app/talaHealth)**

---

## What It Does

AI-powered virtual care platform that provides 24/7 patient access through conversational AI, integrates licensed clinicians for diagnosis, and automates complete care coordination from symptom to resolution.

**Example:** Patient with cough → 24/7 AI intake (2 min) → Dynamic symptom questioning → Pulls medical history → AI generates SOAP note → Matches with NP → Video consultation (8 min) → AI handles insurance verification, lab orders, referrals → Resolution in 4 hours vs 7 days traditional

---

## Why It Matters

**Problem:** Healthcare is slow and fragmented - patients wait days for appointments, navigate confusing processes, face repeated paperwork  
**Solution:** Multi-agent AI system automates intake, triage, coordination while keeping clinicians in the loop for critical decisions

**ROI:** 95% faster time to care, 77% cost reduction, 82% first-contact resolution rate
