## What This Does

AI browser copilot that automates research, manages tabs, summarizes pages, and boosts productivity. Research in 8.5 seconds what would take 45 minutes manually.

**Features:**
- AI-powered research (20 sources analyzed in 8.5s)
- Smart tab management and organization
- Automatic page summarization
- Form auto-fill and price tracking
- 18.2 hours saved per user daily
- 4.8/5 user satisfaction

**Example:** Research "top AI infrastructure companies" → Dex analyzes 20 sources → Extracts funding, products, key info → Generates summary with citations → 8.5 seconds total (vs 45 min manual)
