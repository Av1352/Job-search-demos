## What This Does

AI platform that automates healthcare document processing including prior authorizations, insurance verifications, medical records requests with 98.9% accuracy in 1.7 minutes.

**Features:**
- Automated document processing (5 types)
- 98.9% extraction accuracy
- 95% time reduction (1.7 min vs 31 min)
- Auto-field completion (94.5% rate)
- Insurance verification integration
- HIPAA-compliant workflows

**Example:** Upload prior authorization form → AI extracts 24 fields (patient, diagnosis, procedure, insurance) → Auto-fills form → Validates insurance → 2.3 minutes total (vs 45 min manual) → 98.9% accuracy
